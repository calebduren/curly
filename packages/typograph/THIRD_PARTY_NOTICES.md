# Third-party material

`typeset.css` is adapted from shadcn/ui Typeset, revision `3ba91b1cc83e1bbe4ab35a422ff2a694849c5048`, retrieved September 11, 2026.

Source: https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/3ba91b1cc83e1bbe4ab35a422ff2a694849c5048/apps/v4/app/(app)/(typeset)/typeset.css

Changes: disable font synthesis and small-cap presentation; preserve sentence case and normal tracking for h6; remove the automatic small-screen font-size multiplier so size is explicit; supply independent heading/monospace fallbacks; use a one-pixel blockquote rule. Typograph presets and utilities live separately in `typography.css`. The original theme variables, low-specificity opt-outs and forward-flow spacing remain.
